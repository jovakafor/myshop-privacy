myShop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short

myShop keeps your shop records on your own phone. We do not collect them, we cannot see them, and we do not sell or share them. There is no account and no sign-in.

Who we are

myShop (package name com.jovakafor.shopapp) is made by JOVA LTD. The creator is Joel Jeka.

Contact: jovakafor@gmail.com

What the app stores

When you use myShop, you type in information about your shop. This can include:

  • your shop name
  • your products, prices and categories
  • your sales, stock and expenses
  • your settings, such as the theme and currency

All of this is saved on your phone, inside the app. It is not sent to us or to any server.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information. myShop does not ask you to create an account. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It does not show ads.

When your data leaves your phone

Your records only leave your phone when you choose to send them. For example, when you make a PDF or Excel report, print, or share a shop data file (.sdata) with another phone, for example through WhatsApp. The file goes to the app or person you pick. We do not see it. Please think about who you send it to.

Third-party services

myShop includes the tool it needs to make Excel files. If that built-in copy cannot be used, the app may load the same tool from the cdnjs.cloudflare.com service. If that happens, that service can see your phone's internet address, like any website you visit. No shop records are sent in this request.

The app has Android's internet permission, but it does not upload your records anywhere.

Keeping your data safe and removing it

Your records stay on your phone, so please keep your phone safe. If you uninstall myShop or clear its data, your records are deleted. We cannot get them back for you, so please export a copy from the app before you do this.

Children

myShop is made for shop owners. It is not made for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will put the new version on this page and change the effective date.
